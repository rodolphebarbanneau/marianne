MARIANNE

Your rights, your formalities,
your services

Marianne is a citizen assistant for your computer, for people living in France. It helps you understand your rights, prepare your formalities and keep up with your deadlines, with the AI you already have: Claude Code, Codex or Mistral Vibe.

02 · Features

What Marianne does

One window for the paperwork: ask your question the way it comes, Marianne takes care of the rest, with you.

DOSSIERS

One subject, one folder

Each question becomes a folder: the conversation, the documents and the steps in one place. Snoozed, it comes back on its own on the day you chose.

DÉMARCHES

Guided formalities

Declaring your income, renewing your ID card, appealing a fine… Marianne walks you through them step by step, from reviewed, sourced guides.

ACCORD

You decide

Anything that changes your profile, fills in a form, signs, pays or sends waits for your approval: on a card, or ahead, as you chose. Signing, paying and sending, always on a card.

NAVIGATEUR

Official sites, together

Marianne opens the formalities’ websites in its own browser. You can take over at any time, and it forgets everything when the archive locks.

INFOS

Who covers it, how they headline it

3615 INFOS gathers your newspapers’ headlines into subjects, each source in the colour of its editorial line. The article is read at the newspaper, and your choice of sources stays on your computer.

ÉCHÉANCES

Your deadlines, tasks that come back

Marianne reminds you of what’s coming: a payment, a car inspection, a tax return. A planned task comes back on its day, for a reminder or a check it makes for you, like unpaid tolls: it looks and tells you, it never pays.

03 · Privacy

Nothing leaves your computer.

Marianne has no server and no online account. Everything you give it fits in an encrypted archive: a single file, on your disk, that only you can open.

  • ✓An encrypted archive (AES-256-GCM), opened with your passphrase, then with a simple PIN on this computer.
  • ✓The engine never sees your identifiers: social security number, tax number, name, address, IBAN become tokens.
  • ✓Tokens turn back into your real values on your screen only.
  • ✓The code is open, under the MIT license: anyone can check what it does.

04 · Engines

Your AI, your subscription.

Marianne has no AI of its own: it lends its tools to the one you already have, with your account at its maker. The engine works in an empty folder, with no terminal and no access to your files: it only goes through Marianne.

05 · Services

The Services, code 3615.

Small public-information tools, with their codes, as in the days of the Minitel. They aren’t tied to a folder, and anyone can write one.

3615 METEO

Weather

the weather of your commune, now, hour by hour and over 15 days (Météo-France)

3615 PEAGE

Free-flow tolls

barrier-free motorways: your trips, the 72-hour deadline, the official payment

3615 INFOS

The news

the news of your sources, grouped into subjects: who covers it, how each one headlines it

3615 PARLEMENT

Parliament

the National Assembly and the Senate: your representatives, the votes, the agenda, the sittings on video and live

3615 ANNUAIRE

Public services directory

town hall, Caf, CPAM, tax office, courts near you: addresses, hours, phone numbers

3615 CARTE

Map of France

départements and communes: population, area, density

3615 VOTRE

Yours?

services are open modules, built on an SDK: write the one you’re missing
