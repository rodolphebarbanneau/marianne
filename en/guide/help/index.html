Help

When something goes wrong

  • Forgotten passphrase. On the lock screen, choose « phrase oubliée ? utiliser la clé de secours » (forgot it? use the recovery key), then a new passphrase.
  • The PIN no longer works. After 5 wrong tries, the passphrase is needed again. You can then set a PIN again.
  • Marianne doesn’t answer. Check that your engine is installed and signed in: the lights at the bottom left, and Réglages › Moteurs (Settings › Engines), where « se connecter » (sign in) opens its sign-in. When the engine isn’t signed in, the answer says so, with a button « ouvrir Réglages › Moteurs » (open Settings › Engines). To install one, see Installing Marianne.
  • A planned task didn’t run. It only runs while Marianne is open and your archive unlocked, at the first opening of its day or after it, and never while Marianne is answering you. Check that it isn’t paused, in Démarches › « tâches planifiées » (planned tasks).
  • The screen isn’t up to date. Reload the window, with ctrl+shift+R (cmd+shift+R on macOS) or the button at the top right. The archive stays open.
  • The archive is full. Make some room in Réglages › Place et emplacement (Settings › Space and location).

In the app, F1 opens the guide, with links to the project.

Reporting a bug

The insect at the top right of the window prepares the report for you. Say what you saw: your engine reads it with Marianne’s diagnostics (its version, your system, the screen you were on, its last errors, the open folder’s course without your messages, the end of its log) and writes a report in English: what happened, how to make it happen again, where it may come from. It works without tools or the web, and reads only that. Untick the analysis for a report of your words and the diagnostics alone.

The report is public. Marianne first takes out what it knows to be personal (your identifiers, your name, your home folder on the computer, email addresses, numbers) and shows it to you in full: you can change anything. Nothing leaves Marianne: « ouvrir sur GitHub » (open on GitHub) opens the project’s bug form in your browser, filled in, and you read it again before publishing it, with a GitHub account.

Writing to us

Open an issue on GitHub: “Bug report” for a problem, “Idea” for an idea, “Content correction” for wrong or outdated information in a formality or to propose a news source, “Feedback” for your thoughts. Don’t write any personal data in it: issues are public.

A security vulnerability is reported privately, never in an issue: see the security policy.

Testing the alpha

Thanks for testing Marianne. This is an alpha version: it’s here to find what’s wrong before a real release.

  • Things will break. A later alpha might not read this one’s archive, so don’t put anything in it you couldn’t enter again.
  • Mistral Vibe hasn’t been tried with a real installation yet.

What we’d like you to try:

  1. Create your archive: the passphrase, then the recovery key. Write it down.
  2. Set a PIN to reopen the archive quickly, on this computer.
  3. Fill in a bit of your profile (Réglages › Moi), if you like: nothing leaves your computer, and the engine never sees your numbers, only tokens.
  4. Ask Marianne a real question, about a formality you have in progress.
  5. Follow a guided formality (Démarches › an area).
  6. Browse the Services: 3615 INFOS, METEO, CARTE, PARLEMENT, ANNUAIRE, PEAGE.
