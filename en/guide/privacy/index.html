Your data

On your computer

Everything you give Marianne, your folders, your documents and your profile, is in your archive, encrypted with your passphrase. Marianne has no server and no online account: nothing is sent to it.

What the engine sees

The engine, Claude Code, Codex or Mistral Vibe, works with your account at its maker. It receives the text of your conversations and what it needs to help you, but never your identifiers in clear: social security number, tax number, name, address, IBAN… are replaced by tokens, and put back in place only on your screen. The maker’s terms apply to what it receives. It never receives your political leaning, your themes or your news sources.

What goes online

Marianne fetches public information (the newspapers’ headlines, the services’ data) from its public sources, opens the formalities’ websites in its browser, and downloads its updates from GitHub. These requests carry nothing personal, except what you agreed to: the part of your profile a service asked you for, like your license plate for the tolls, and what Marianne fills in for you on a website, with your approval. A planned task reaches the sites you gave it without asking again, and types there what its sheet says, like your license plate for the tolls. A bug report leaves only from your browser, when you publish it on GitHub (see Help).

The news

For 3615 INFOS, Marianne reads the feeds of every newspaper on its list, not only those you accepted: no newspaper can tell which ones you read. It keeps their headlines for 7 days. It reads the start of an article (a few lines) only for the headline shown, when you show it, and never past what a newspaper keeps for its subscribers: the rest is read on its site.

Your news sources are part of your profile, like your leaning: what you read says what you think. The engine never receives them, and no service reads them without your consent, never given in advance (Réglages › Identité › qui peut les lire, who may read them). A service that may not read them gets every newspaper on the list.

Community services

A community service (a .3615 file, or the community catalogue once it opens) runs apart: it sees neither your folders, nor your documents, nor your identifiers, connects only to the sites it declares, and knows only the commune of your address. « ✓ validé » (validated) means the project reviewed this version; « non validé » (not validated) and « ⚠ hors catalogue » (outside the catalogue), that no one did: add only what you know. When Marianne uses its tools in a folder, it sends them what they need to answer you, never more than a short message. For a service that isn’t « ✓ validé », it first shows you what would leave, every time: nothing goes without your approval.

Backing up

In Réglages › Place et emplacement (Settings › Space and location):

  • copie de sauvegarde (backup copy): an encrypted copy of the archive, to keep elsewhere, on a USB key or another disk.
  • tout exporter (export everything): your documents, your conversations and your profile, as ordinary, unencrypted files.

The same page shows the space left. Marianne warns you when the archive is 80% full. Once full, it no longer accepts new documents.

Without the passphrase or the recovery key, nobody can open the archive again, not even us: keep your recovery key somewhere safe.
