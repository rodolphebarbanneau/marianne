# Using Marianne

## Folders

A folder is a conversation with Marianne about one subject: a formality, a question, a letter to understand. Write on the home page to start one (ctrl+enter opens a new one from anywhere). « + pièce » attaches a document, and the menu next to the « envoyer » (send) button chooses the folder's engine.

Ongoing folders are in the left column, with « chercher » (search) at the top to find one. Those you put on hold come back on the date you chose, and those you file stay within reach, under « Classés » (filed).

## Your approval

Anything that changes your profile, fills in a form, signs, pays or sends goes through you first: Marianne shows it to you on a card, and waits for your approval.

## The built-in browser

Marianne opens the sites it needs in the folder's browser, on the right (the « navigateur » button). It asks for your approval before going online, and always before signing, paying or sending. You can take over (« prendre la main ») at any time. This browser forgets everything when the archive locks.

## The pages

- **Démarches** (formalities): the areas of your administrative life (taxes, health, housing, family…), each with its dashboard and its guided formalities, step by step.
- **Services**: the 3615 codes, INFOS, METEO, PEAGE, CARTE, PARLEMENT, ANNUAIRE. Type a code or words in the 3615 bar to find one. The star pins a service as a tile, at the top of « mes services » (my services), with what's new for you: today's weather, a toll to pay. « + découvrir » (discover) offers the official services you haven't added yet, then the community's. Before you add a service, Marianne shows you what it will be able to do. A service that needs part of your profile, like your license plate for the tolls, asks you first.

## The news

3615 INFOS, among your services, shows the headlines of your sources over the last 24 hours, grouped into subjects: a subject is the headlines of different newspapers that say the same thing, ranked by how many sources cover it. « à la une » (front page, F2) shows the list and the chosen subject, the « fil » (wire, F3) the same headlines by time, the « kiosque » (newsstand, F4) source by source. « / » goes to the search field, at the top.

Each source has its colour, its editorial line, from the left (red) to the right (blue), grey without a classification. The one Marianne gives is its default classification, and you can change it. The colours don't sort or hide anything. You choose your sources in Réglages › Identité › sources d’information (news sources): accept or set aside (« accepter · écarter ») a newspaper, and its sections with ⚙. The list is Marianne's, the same for everyone.

## The Parliament's videos

In 3615 PARLEMENT, the vidéos tab opens the sittings and committees of the Assemblée nationale and the Sénat in Marianne's player, with the agenda beside it.

- **The player**: the controls are over the picture and fade while it plays. The track shows the agenda's main points and, in black, the suspensions of the sitting; hovered, the moment, the subject and the speaker. After the position, the current subject: a click shows it in the agenda, which follows the playback. A click on a line of the agenda goes there.
- **The options** (the gear): the quality, the speed, and the time, the video's or the hémicycle's clock (the agenda follows this choice).
- **Keeping a clip** (« garder un extrait », in the options, or X): the track narrows around the passage; set its start and end (I and O at the position), « caler sur l’intervention » takes the speaker's, then « garder ». Your clips are in vidéos › extraits, private, in your archive: to watch them again, share them or talk about them with Marianne.
- **Sharing the link** (« partager le lien »): a link to the video's official page that anyone can open, at its start, at the chosen moment or at the start of the clip. « copier avec le titre » adds the subject, the speaker, the chamber, the day and the time.
- **Live**: a sitting on air opens at the live edge, and the track goes back to its opening; its agenda fills in as it goes (« ● en cours » marks the latest point). DIRECT, or the End key, goes back to live. Once the sitting is over, the page moves to the recorded video as soon as the portal has it ready, at the same place. Until the portal offers a live's video, only its last seconds can be watched.
- **The mini-player** (P): the video goes on in a corner of the window while you do something else in Marianne; it also opens when you leave the page of a video that is playing. Drag it to a corner; a click on its caption brings you back to the video's page, where it is.

## Your profile

In Réglages › Moi (Settings › Me), you can fill in your identity, your household and your documents. It's optional: it helps Marianne prepare your formalities. See [Your data](privacy.md) for what the engine sees of it.

In Réglages › Identité, you may also say which political groups you feel close to, which themes matter to you, and your news sources, if you wish. Your leaning only serves to compare: 3615 PARLEMENT tells you whether your representatives vote as your ideas, without sorting or hiding anything. Your themes bring forward the headlines and the ballots about them. You choose which services may read them, and see when they did.

## The keys

Like on a Minitel, the bottom bar shows the screen's keys:

| Key        | What it does                | Shortcut   |
| ---------- | --------------------------- | ---------- |
| ENVOI      | send                        | enter      |
| ANNULATION | stop Marianne, close        | esc        |
| CORRECTION | edit my last message        | ↑          |
| RÉPÉTITION | redo the answer             | ctrl+R     |
| SUITE      | next page                   | page down  |
| RETOUR     | previous page               | page up    |
| SOMMAIRE   | the app's menu              | ctrl+space |
| GUIDE      | help, links, your shortcuts | F1         |

Also: ctrl+K for commands, ctrl+alt+space to search everywhere, ctrl+, for settings, ctrl+shift+R to reload the window. In a service, F2, F3… open its tabs, and F1 its guide. On macOS, cmd replaces ctrl, except for the menu and search. All of them can be changed in Réglages › Raccourcis clavier (Settings › Keyboard shortcuts).
