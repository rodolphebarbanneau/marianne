# Using Marianne

## Folders

A folder is a conversation with Marianne about one subject: a formality, a question, a letter to understand. Write on the home page to start one (ctrl+enter opens a new one from anywhere). « + pièce » attaches a document, and the menu next to the « envoyer » (send) button chooses the folder's engine.

Ongoing folders are in the left column, with « chercher » (search) at the top to find one. Those you put on hold come back on the date you chose, and those you file stay within reach, under « Classés » (filed). A folder is also filed by itself when its guided formality is done, or after 7 days without activity when it follows none; a pinned folder, or one holding a planned task, never is. A new message brings it back.

## What Marianne does for you

Ask it for something precise (a sum owed, a form to fill in, a document to obtain, a formality with a deadline): Marianne gets to work. It looks for the service and the guided formality that cover it, asks you to fill in what is missing, goes online and prepares everything up to your decision; when it comes back regularly, it offers a planned task. For a broader situation (a life event, a general question), it first shows you the formalities and services that help, and creates nothing until you have chosen. What needs your approval, it asks on a card, without asking it again in the conversation.

When one of Marianne's official services would help and isn't among your services (or you switched it off), it offers it on a card: what it does, the sites it connects to, those where Marianne would act for you with it (a payment page, for instance), what it would read of your profile, the tools it lends Marianne. « voir avant d’ajouter » (see before adding) opens the same window as the Services page, and you are the one who adds it; if it needs part of your profile, it asks you right away. Marianne then carries on by itself, and the conversation says so on a line (« 3615 PEAGE ajouté · Marianne reprend », 3615 PEAGE added · Marianne carries on). « pas maintenant » (not now): it carries on without, and doesn't offer it again in that folder. It never offers a community service: those, you find yourself on the Services page.

## Your approval

Anything that changes your profile, fills in a form, signs, pays or sends goes through you first: on a card, where Marianne waits for your approval, or ahead, by what you choose below.

In Réglages › Autorisations (Settings › Permissions), you choose what it may do without asking:

- **« tout demander »** (ask everything): each new site of each folder, each form and each save to your profile is asked.
- **« équilibré »** (balanced, the default): going online is asked once per folder, and each form and each save too.
- **« autonomie »** (autonomy): Marianne goes without asking to the sites it knows (the public sites of its list, and the sites its official services act on, once you have switched them on), fills in forms there and saves your deadlines; other sites are asked once per folder.

On a card, between « approuver » (approve) and « refuser » (refuse), « toujours pour sanef.com » (always for sanef.com), « toujours pour les formulaires de sanef.com » (always for sanef.com's forms) or « toujours pour les échéances » (always for deadlines) spares you the same question next time, in all your folders. These permissions are in Réglages › Autorisations, with the day you gave them, and « retirer » (remove) to take them back. What Marianne does this way stays written in the conversation, on an « autorisé d’office » (allowed ahead) line, with the values of the form or the deadline. The button of a planned task that offers a next step (« payer maintenant », pay now) is an approval too, for that one answer: Marianne goes to the task's sites and fills in forms on its service's sites without asking, except at « tout demander », and the line says so (« autorisé d’office · payer maintenant »).

Paying, signing, sending, submitting, declaring, certifying and deleting always wait for your yes, on a card, every time, whatever you chose; paying, on the payment card ([Paying by card](#paying-by-card)). On the sites where one of your official services acts, Marianne clicks the steps on the way by itself (searching, validating, continuing, going on to pay); going there and filling in a form there follow your choice above. The payment and what commits you otherwise (signing, sending, declaring, deleting, certifying, subscribing, cancelling a contract…) are always asked. When the page there holds a card's fields, or once Marianne signed in to your account there, or you took over or typed something there, until the archive locks, every button there that commits you (« payer », « valider », « confirmer », « commander »…) is asked, except the one click your yes on the payment card covers; otherwise, a click that would pay there without the payment card is asked only if the engine recognises it as such. Typing one of your identifiers on a site, your license plate for instance, is asked as a form on that site is: on a card, unless you allowed its forms ahead or a task's next step covers it. Only deadlines can be saved to your profile without asking: your identity, your numbers, your address, your accounts, your ID documents, your household and your entities are asked every time. Planned tasks never use these permissions.

## The built-in browser

Marianne opens the sites it needs in the folder's browser, on the right (the « navigateur », browser, button); drag its left edge to widen or narrow it, and double-click that edge to put its default width back. It asks for your approval before going online, as you chose ([Your approval](#your-approval)), and always before signing, paying or sending; refusing a site's cookies (« tout refuser »), it does without asking. You can take over (« prendre la main ») at any time. When it needs you (FranceConnect, a code received by text message), it opens the folder's browser; if you are on another screen, a message tells you, and the browser opens when you come back to the folder. The rest of the time, Réglages › Navigateur › « afficher le navigateur » (Settings › Browser › show the browser) decides: « quand Marianne le propose » (when Marianne offers), « toujours » (always), or « jamais, il travaille hors de vue » (never, it works out of sight). This browser forgets everything when the archive locks.

## Paying by card

In Réglages › Paiement (Settings › Payment), you can save your bank cards: a name to tell it apart (« perso »), the holder, the number and the expiry date. Marianne checks the number as you type it, then only ever shows it masked (« Visa •••• 4242 · perso · 09/28 »): to change it, you type it again. The security code (« cryptogramme ») isn't saved, unless you tick « enregistrer le cryptogramme » (save the security code) for that card: it's simpler, but whoever opened your archive would then have everything needed to pay with it.

When something must be paid on a site it knows (a public site of its list, or a site where one of your official services acts), or on the payment page a site of your official services sends you to (going there then counts as going to that site), Marianne shows you a payment card: the site, the page, the card, the amount it read on the page and what it will fill in. If the security code isn't saved, you type it in that card: it's used for that payment only. Once you « approuver » (approve), it fills your card in on the page, then presses the pay button: the same yes covers that one click, on that page, for a few minutes, and a second click on a pay button of that page is asked again. Your bank's page that follows (3-D Secure) is part of that payment: Marianne goes there without asking you again, and hands over to you for the confirmation (a code, its app). On another site, or when it can't tell which field is which, it fills nothing: you type your card yourself.

Neither the number nor the security code reaches the engine: all it knows of your cards is their name, brand, last 4 digits and expiry. A planned task never pays.

## The pages

- **Démarches** (formalities): the areas of your administrative life (taxes, health, housing, family…), each with its own page and its guided formalities, step by step.
- **Services**: the 3615 codes, INFOS, METEO, PEAGE, CARTE, PARLEMENT, ANNUAIRE. 3615 METEO gives your commune's weather: now, the rain of the next two hours, 48 hours, 7 and 15 days, with air quality, UV and pollen. Type a code or words in the 3615 bar to find one. The star pins a service as a tile, at the top of « mes services » (my services, F2), with what's new for you: today's weather, a toll to pay. « découvrir » (discover, F3) offers the official services you haven't added yet, then the community's. Before you add a service, Marianne shows you what it will be able to do, and the sites where Marianne will act for you with that service: it goes through the steps there without asking, but paying, signing or sending waits for your yes, and going there or filling in a form follows your permissions ([Your approval](#your-approval)); a link of Marianne's to an official service you haven't added opens that window. A service that needs part of your profile, like your license plate for the tolls, asks you first.

## The news

3615 INFOS, among your services, shows the headlines of your sources over the last 24 hours, grouped into subjects: a subject is the headlines of different newspapers that say the same thing, ranked by how many sources cover it. « à la une » (front page, F2) shows the list and the chosen subject, the « fil » (wire, F3) the same headlines by time, the « kiosque » (newsstand, F4) source by source. « / » goes to the search field, at the top.

Each source has its colour, its editorial line, from the left (red) to the right (blue), a greyish white at the centre, for a source with no clear lean. The one Marianne gives is its default classification, and you can change it. The colours don't sort or hide anything. You choose your sources in Réglages › Identité › sources d’information (news sources): accept (« accepter ») or set aside (« écarter ») a newspaper, and choose its sections with ⚙. The list is Marianne's, the same for everyone.

## The Parliament's videos

In 3615 PARLEMENT, the vidéos (videos) tab opens the sittings and committees of the Assemblée nationale and the Sénat in Marianne's player, with the agenda beside it.

- **The player**: the controls are over the picture and fade while it plays. The track shows the agenda's main points and, in black, the suspensions of the sitting; hovered, the moment, the subject and the speaker. After the position, the current subject: a click shows it in the agenda, which follows the playback. A click on a line of the agenda goes there.
- **Full screen** (F, the button on the right, or a double click in the middle of the picture): the video takes the whole screen, and esc leaves it. O shows the agenda, leaving full screen. On the keyboard too: space plays or pauses, ← and → go back or forward 10 seconds, ↑ and ↓ set the volume, M mutes it.
- **The options** (the gear): the quality, the speed, the time, the video's or the hémicycle's clock (the agenda follows this choice), and picture-in-picture (« image dans l’image »).
- **Keeping a clip** (« garder un extrait », in the options, or X): the track narrows around the passage; set its start and end (I and O at the position), « caler sur l’intervention » (fit to the speech) takes the speaker's, then « garder l’extrait » (keep the clip, enter); « annuler » (cancel) or esc drops it. Your clips are in vidéos › extraits (videos › clips), private, in your archive: to watch them again, share them or talk about them with Marianne.
- **Sharing the link** (« partager le lien »): a link to the video's official page that anyone can open, at its start, at the chosen moment or at the start of the clip. « copier avec le titre » (copy with the title) adds the subject, the speaker, the chamber, the day and the time.
- **Live**: a sitting on air opens at the live edge, and the track goes back to its opening; its agenda fills in as it goes (« ● en cours », in progress, marks the latest point). DIRECT (live), or the End key, goes back to live. Once the sitting is over, the page moves to the recorded video as soon as the portal has it ready, at the same place. Until the portal offers a live's video, only its last seconds can be watched.
- **The mini-player** (P): the video goes on in a corner of the window while you do something else in Marianne; it also opens when you leave the page of a video that is playing. Drag it to a corner by its grip, top left; a click or a drag along its track moves through the video, and a click on its caption brings you back to the video's page, where it is. Another video that starts playing takes its place (opening a video's page without playing it doesn't), and it closes if you turn off or remove its service.

## The Démarches page

The bar at the top asks « QUELLE DÉMARCHE ? » (which formality?). Each area is a numbered counter, a « guichet »: type its number and the ticket machine prints your ticket, 08 for Mobilité, then ENVOI (enter) takes you there. Type words instead to search everything at once: your folders, documents and deadlines, the guided formalities, the official links and the areas. ↑ and ↓ move through the results, enter opens one, esc clears the search, and the last line always offers to ask Marianne.

Under the bar, three tabs, on F2 to F4:

- **mes domaines** (my areas): what presses, then one line per area, with what waits for you or its next deadline, and the eight weeks to come. Each deadline is a square, red when it's late or within 3 days.
- **à faire** (to do): everything, in order: what's late, what waits for you, what falls within 3 days, then the next 45 days.
- **tâches planifiées** (planned tasks): all your planned tasks, each with its switch.

## An area's page

An area's page has the same head, with its counter's number: Mobilité is GUICHET 8 (counter 8). The ← arrow at the top, or esc, goes back to Démarches, and the bar takes a question: Marianne answers it in a new folder, filed in this area. Four tabs, on F2 to F5:

- **aperçu** (overview): questions to start from, what's to do here, your vehicles or homes, the weeks to come, the services that can help, your folders and tasks.
- **tâches planifiées** (planned tasks): each task in full, then those your services propose.
- **dossiers** (folders): those in progress, on hold and filed, and the area's guided formalities.
- **ressources** (resources): your documents, Service-Public sheets, official links.

## Planned tasks

A planned task is a folder that comes back. Marianne can check something for you on a regular rhythm, like unpaid tolls every 2 days, or simply bring a reminder back on its day. Between two times the folder sleeps under « En veille » (on hold); on its day, it comes back.

A task starts from « + nouvelle tâche » (new task), from what your services propose on an area's page, or from a conversation, when Marianne offers one. It never starts without your yes, on a sheet or a card that says what it does, how often, and which sites it may reach. When one of your services proposes that task, Marianne's card shows what its sheet would show, and the task takes everything from the service, never from Marianne; it comes back in the conversation's folder, or in a new folder if that one already has its task.

- **A day, not a time.** A check runs at the first opening of its day, or the first one after it. It only runs while Marianne is open and your archive unlocked, it waits for Marianne to finish what it's doing for you, and it gives way as soon as you ask something.
- **It can't protect you if you don't open Marianne.** A toll must be paid within 72 hours: a few days without opening Marianne, and a passage can go past its deadline.
- **It spends your engine.** Each check uses your engine, as a question would, with at most 12 checks a day. A reminder costs nothing.
- **A check never pays.** It can only look and report: it doesn't pay, sign or send, and nothing it reads on a page can make it. It reaches the sites you gave it, for that check only, and no other. On those the service that proposes it declares, it can carry the search through, even when their buttons say « payer » (pay) or « valider » (validate): with no card and no sign-in, it can't pay. If a site wants a code, or anything else needs you, it stops and tells you.

When a check finds nothing, the folder goes back to sleep, and its conversation folds these checks into one line: click it to read them. When it finds something or needs you, the folder comes back and a notification tells you, without saying which task: a locked screen may show it. What waits for you also shows in « à faire ».

A service's task may offer a next step, which its sheet announces (« si elle trouve quelque chose : payer maintenant », if it finds something: pay now). When a check finds something, the folder offers it as a button, on that check's line, and « à faire » opens the folder there. One click, and Marianne sees to it with you, in the conversation, as if you had asked. That click is your approval for that answer: it goes to the task's sites and fills in forms on its service's sites without asking you (except at « tout demander »). What commits you still waits for your approval, on a card: for a toll, the payment card, the one confirmation to give. The button disappears while the task is paused or its service is switched off, and once the next step has started; it comes back if that failed or was stopped.

In a task's folder, « modifier la tâche » (edit the task) changes its rhythm or removes it, and « mettre en pause » (pause) stops it until you choose « reprendre » (resume). While it's paused, its folder is under « Classés » (filed).

## Your profile

In Réglages › Moi (Settings › Me), you can fill in your identity, your household, your documents and your payment cards ([Paying by card](#paying-by-card)). It's optional: it helps Marianne prepare your formalities. See [Your data](privacy.md) for what the engine sees of it.

In Réglages › Identité, you may also say which political groups you feel close to, which themes matter to you, and your news sources, if you wish. Your leaning only serves to compare: 3615 PARLEMENT tells you whether your representatives vote as your ideas, without sorting or hiding anything. Your themes bring forward the headlines and the ballots about them. You choose which services may read them, and see when they did.

## The keys

Like on a Minitel, the bottom bar shows the screen's keys:

| Key        | What it does                | Shortcut   |
| ---------- | --------------------------- | ---------- |
| ENVOI      | send                        | enter      |
| ANNULATION | stop Marianne, close        | esc        |
| CORRECTION | edit my last message        | ↑          |
| RÉPÉTITION | redo the answer             | ctrl+R     |
| SUITE      | next page                   | page down  |
| RETOUR     | previous page               | page up    |
| SOMMAIRE   | the app's menu              | ctrl+space |
| GUIDE      | help, links, your shortcuts | F1         |

The bar follows the screen: on a service's screen or an area's page, RETOUR (esc) goes back, and in the settings, ENVOI (ctrl+S) saves.

Also: ctrl+K for commands, ctrl+alt+space to search everywhere, ctrl+2 and ctrl+3 for Démarches and Services, ctrl+, for settings, ctrl+B to fold the left column, F11 for full screen, ctrl+shift+R to reload the window. In a service, F2, F3… open its tabs, F1 its guide, and « / » its search; on Démarches, Services and an area's page, F2, F3… open their tabs. On macOS, cmd replaces ctrl, except for the menu and search, and full screen is ctrl+cmd+F. All of them can be changed in Réglages › Raccourcis clavier (Settings › Keyboard shortcuts).
