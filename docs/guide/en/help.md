# Help

## When something goes wrong

- **Forgotten passphrase.** On the lock screen, choose « phrase oubliée ? utiliser la clé de secours » (forgot it? use the recovery key), then a new passphrase.
- **The PIN no longer works.** After 5 wrong tries, the passphrase is needed again. You can then set a PIN again.
- **Marianne doesn't answer.** Check that your engine is installed and signed in: the lights at the bottom left, and Réglages › Moteurs (Settings › Engines).
- **The screen isn't up to date.** Reload the window, with ctrl+shift+R (cmd+shift+R on macOS) or the button at the top right. The archive stays open.
- **The archive is full.** Make some room in Réglages › Place et emplacement (Settings › Space and location).

In the app, F1 opens the guide, with links to the project.

## Writing to us

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/rodolphebarbanneau/marianne/issues/new/choose): "Bug report" for a problem, "Idea" for an idea, "Content correction" for wrong or outdated information in a formality, "Feedback" for your thoughts. Don't write **any personal data** in it: issues are public.

A security vulnerability is reported privately, never in an issue: see the [security policy](https://github.com/rodolphebarbanneau/marianne/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Testing the alpha

Thanks for testing Marianne. This is an alpha version: it's here to find what's wrong before a real release.

- Things will break. A later alpha might not read this one's archive, so don't put anything in it you couldn't enter again.
- Mistral Vibe hasn't been tried with a real installation yet.

What we'd like you to try:

1. Create your archive: the passphrase, then the **recovery key**. Write it down.
2. Set a PIN to reopen the archive quickly, on this computer.
3. Fill in a bit of your profile (Réglages › Moi), if you like: nothing leaves your computer, and the engine never sees your numbers, only tokens.
4. Ask Marianne a real question, about a formality you have in progress.
5. Follow a guided formality (Démarches › an area).
6. Browse the news (Actualités) and the Services: 3615 METEO, CARTE, PARLEMENT, ANNUAIRE, PEAGE.
